Celebrity: Get a Clue

The party game where teams race to get their teammates to guess celebrity names. Give any clue you like in Round One—but in Round Two, you're down to just one word and one gesture per name. Names span sports, movies, music, history, and more, across four difficulty levels.

What's included

110 of the most widely recognized Level 1 names are included free. The full Level 1 set and Levels 2, 3, and 4 are available as optional in-app purchases—Level 4 names are the most obscure, for players who are ready for a real challenge. You can purchase Level 1 only, Levels 1 and 2, or all four levels; upgrade pricing is available if you've already purchased lower levels.
