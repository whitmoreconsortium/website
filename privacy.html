Privacy Policy

Whitmore Consortium  ·  Last updated: June 20, 2026

Overview

Celebrity: Get a Clue ("the app") is developed and published by Whitmore Consortium. This policy describes how we handle information in connection with the app.

Information We Collect

The app does not directly collect personally identifiable information. The app uses the following third-party services, each of which may collect information as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Analytics (Google)—collects anonymized usage data such as which screens are viewed and which features are used, to help us understand how the app is being used. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google)—collects crash reports and diagnostic data when the app encounters an error, to help us identify and fix bugs. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring (Google)—collects performance data such as app startup time and network response times, to help us identify and fix performance issues. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google)—used to deliver configuration updates to the app without requiring an app update. No personal data is collected through this service. Firebase Privacy Policy

In-App Purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases processed through Google Play Billing. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Google. We do not receive or store your payment information. Google's privacy policy applies to all payment processing: policies.google.com/privacy .

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects when the policy was last revised. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at privacy@whitmoreconsortium.com.
