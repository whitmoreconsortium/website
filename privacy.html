Privacy Policy

Whitmore Consortium  ·  Last updated: July 18, 2026

Overview

Celebrity: Get a Clue ("the app") is developed and published by Whitmore Consortium. This policy describes what information is collected in connection with the app and how it is used.

Device identifier

The app generates a random identifier when first launched and stores it on your device. This identifier is not linked to your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information. It is used to associate analytics events and crash reports from the same device, and to attribute correction submissions (see below).

Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data, including app session information, feature usage, and the device identifier described above. This data helps us understand how the app is used and improve it over time. Firebase Analytics is operated by Google; their privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Crash reports

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash reports and non-fatal error information. Reports include device model, OS version, app version, and the device identifier described above. This data is used solely to identify and fix bugs. Crashlytics is operated by Google; their privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Correction submissions

The app allows you to voluntarily submit corrections to name or clue data. When you submit a correction, the following information is sent to our servers and stored:

  • The name and clue text you are correcting
  • The type(s) of correction selected
  • Any optional comment you choose to include
  • Your device identifier
  • App version and submission timestamp

Correction submissions are stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore and used only to review and act on reported issues. We do not use them for any other purpose.

In-app purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases processed through Google Play Billing. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Google. We do not receive or store your payment information. Google's privacy policy applies to all payment processing: policies.google.com/privacy.

Data sharing

We do not sell or transfer your information to third parties, except as necessary to operate the services described above (Firebase, Google Play).

Data retention

Analytics and crash report data is retained according to Firebase's default retention settings. Correction submissions are retained indefinitely for reference, but may be deleted upon request.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects when the policy was last revised. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at privacy@whitmoreconsortium.com.
